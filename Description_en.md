# AutoFishS 🎣
A fishing helper mod for **StarCatcher**.

![Forge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Forge-47.4.0-orange) ![Side](https://img.shields.io/badge/Side-Client--Only-blue)

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Minigame auto-hit
When the fishing minigame appears, it automatically hits as soon as the pointer reaches a sweet spot. Supports `AUTO` (any hit counts) and `PERFECT` (aim for perfect) modes.  
Includes smart strategies like treasure focus and skipping negative spots (frozen/explosive). If the auto-hitter ever misses, you can also adjust the perfect leeway angle to fix it.

### 🪝 Auto-reel & auto-cast
Reels in automatically the moment a fish bites, then casts again after each catch — AFK fishing without staring at the screen.

### 🚫 Fishing blacklist
Don't want certain fish? Press **F7** to open the blacklist screen and click a fish to blacklist/unblacklist it. Item-icon grid with search support; red slots mean blacklisted — blacklisted fish are skipped automatically.

### 🧩 Fishing Overhaul compatibility
When Fishing Overhaul is installed, its fishing minigame is also supported.

## 🎮 Default controls
- **F8**: open the settings screen
- **F7**: open the fishing blacklist

## ⚠️ Notes
- Does not activate during tournaments, and does not apply to fish you haven't unlocked yet.
- May affect vanilla fishing rod behavior.
